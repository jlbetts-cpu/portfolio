# Developmental Improvisation — Design System

> Import into Notion: Settings & members → Import → Markdown & CSV, or drop this file onto a page. Tables import as tables; the quoted lines become callouts.

## Principles

> Premium is subtraction. When a screen feels wrong, remove something before adding something.

> Counting is not looking. Measure, then look at the screenshot.

> One typeface, two weights, no italics, no gradient text.

> Colour lives on shapes and cards. Text is white or a white tier, except inside a coloured card.

> No shadows. No gradients. Depth is a lighter surface plus a hairline.

> Flat vectors only. The star and the figure come from the logo file.

> Motion is a system: six rungs, two loops, two springs. Anything moving longer than five seconds has a pause control.

> Copy is verbatim from the old site. Placeholders are marked.

> 44px targets, measured.

Rules
- One accent per section, set once. Order down the home page: sky, green, yellow, violet, pink, orange, sky, green.
- Full-strength colour only on chips, stars, the focus ring, initials and the four stacked cards.
- Inside a coloured card the ink flips to dark (white on violet). Buttons and chips invert automatically.
- Photographs are never tinted or overlaid. No gradients anywhere.

## Motion

| Rung | Duration | For |
|---|---|---|
| press | 100ms | button press, scale .97 |
| state | 160 / 240ms | hover, focus, colour |
| move | 280ms | position, the stack recede, the pile straighten |
| reveal | 360ms | scroll reveals, the pile drop |
| enter | 500ms | dialog, hero first paint |
| orbit | 96s | the arch and the logo ring, one turn |
| bob | 3.2s | the figures |

> Twelve interactions and no others. Reduced motion: loops freeze, reveals fade, nothing tilts or drops.

## Components

- Button: primary (white), secondary (hairline), ghost, compact 44px. 48px tall, radius 14.
- Chip: 32px pill in the accent with dark text.
- Star: the logo's star, 16px, before every section label.
- Card: raised, tint (16% accent), accent (full colour, radius 28).
- Photo: 4:5, 3:2 or 1:1, radius 20, caption below, never on the image.
- Orbit: 14 cards on a half-circle whose centre sits at the fold; tilt 0.4 × angle; hover pauses; a pause button.
- Figures: the logo figure repeated, arms touching, bobbing 2px.
- Stack: sticky coloured cards; the one beneath tilts back 6°.
- Pile: five tilted testimonial cards that drop in; a scroll row on phones.
- Field: 48px input, pink hairline on error, "Subscribed" with a check on success.
- Dialog: native dialog; modal on desktop, a bottom sheet under 38vh on phones; focus goes to the heading.
- Nav: transparent, then frosted with a hairline after 24px of scroll. Full white mark plus the wordmark.
- Footer: mark, one line, ©, Menu and Contact columns.

## Photographs

- Factual alt text, explicit dimensions, lazy except the first arch cards.
- AVIF, WebP and JPEG at 320, 480 and 960 wide, generated by the pipeline.
- Crops chosen by looking at each at its rendered size.
- Releases for children confirmed with Linda before launch.

## Gates

Nine scripts run serially and print numbers: layout, targets, contrast, copy, images, motion, orbit, dialog, accessibility. The orbit gate has a self-test that injects an overlap and must fail.
