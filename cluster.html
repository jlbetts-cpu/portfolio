Overview

Cluster is a campus app that helps students find clubs they would never stumble on. My first full design project: five weeks, a brand-new tool, a team of three. It is the one that made me fall in love with product design.

Problem

More than 800 clubs, and no way to find them.

UC Davis is home to more than 800 student clubs, but the ones that are not already big are invisible unless you catch the once-a-year club fair. I was a transfer that quarter, new to campus and looking for my people. Every transfer I talked to said the same thing: no idea how many clubs existed, or how to find the niche ones they would actually love.

The build

A first real project, learned in five weeks.

I came to Davis as a communications major and found design through a fellowship built for total beginners. I designed every screen of Cluster in five weeks while teaching myself Figma from zero. My title was product designer, but I took the lead the whole way, and that visit to LinkedIn’s headquarters at the end of the program is the moment I knew this was the work I wanted.

The concept

A home where every club can be found.

Cluster gives every club a place to show up. The Discover feed is catered to your interests, but sometimes you would rather browse by mood, so Sports, Dance, Design and more each get their own feed. Club profiles gather a club’s articles and events in one place, so you feel what being a member is really like, and messaging makes it social, because finding your people should feel like college, not a database.

Outcome

Most Innovative, and the reason design stuck.

Our team won Most Innovative. Looking back from 2026 there is plenty I would change: cut the messaging, tighten the spacing and typography, lean on the kind of design system I now use religiously (Cluster had none, I did not know where to start yet).

There is a footnote, too. In 2026 a friend shipped an app called tapin that does almost exactly what Cluster set out to do, and I helped him polish the UI over coffee, my name is in one of its App Store screenshots. Cluster never shipped, but it is the reason I went all in on design, and that drive runs straight through Bearings, Apollo, and Strata.

Open to full-time roles.
