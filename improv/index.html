Book a workshopWhat is developmental improv?

Not improv for the stage.

Developmental improvisation borrows the games of improv and points them at the person instead of the performance. Nobody is trying to be funny. Everybody is learning to listen, to trust the room, and to find out what they are good at.

  • Fifty years of teaching

    Linda has taught improvisation since the 1970s, to children, teachers, coaches and clinicians, across the United States and abroad.

  • Learning from the inside out

    Social-emotional learning by doing: confidence, cooperation, focus, memory, intuition and trust, built one game at a time.

  • A method, not a bag of games

    The Fulton Method has three phases, and there is a full program for learning to teach it yourself.

Three ways in.

Every workshop starts from the same place: a circle, a game, and permission to get it wrong. Where it goes from there depends on who is in the room.
