A circle, a game, and permission to get it wrong.

Three ways to work with Linda. Every one of them starts the same way and ends with people who trust themselves a little more than they did.

01

Techniques

The exercises of the Fulton Method, taught as tools. Each game is introduced, played, and then taken apart so you can see what it builds and when to reach for it. Dozens of them, from a first name game to full scene work.
